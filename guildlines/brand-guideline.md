## Philosophy

TONELS is an intelligent coordination layer for digital assets. The four modules conntect form a secure vault core at the center symbolizing the unity of security, intelligence, and execution.

### Logo
The TONELS logo is a visual representation of the brand's core values and identity. It combines elements of security, intelligence, and execution to create a distinctive and memorable mark. The logo features a stylized vault, symbolizing security and protection, with interconnected lines representing the intelligent coordination layer. The color palette is designed to evoke trust and professionalism, with a modern and sleek aesthetic.

### Concept Palette
- Obsidian Black — #0E1111
A classic choice. More sophisticated than standard navy or black. Perfect for a wallet that feels secure and reliable.

- Signal Lime — #D6FF3F
A bright, attention-grabbing color that conveys energy and innovation. It adds a modern touch to the brand and can be used for accents and highlights.

- Muted Brass — #B8A77A
A warm, earthy tone that complements the other colors in the palette. It adds a sense of stability and trustworthiness to the brand.

- Bone White — #F4EFE6
A soft, neutral color that provides a clean and minimalist backdrop for the other elements of the brand. It enhances readability and allows the logo and other design elements to stand out.

- Graphite — #3A3F3B
A dark, muted gray that adds depth and sophistication to the brand. It can be used for text and other design elements to create contrast and enhance readability.


### Typography
TONELS uses Space Grotesk as the primary typeface for all branding materials. Space Grotesk is a modern, geometric sans-serif font that conveys a sense of professionalism and innovation. It is clean and legible, making it suitable for both digital and print applications. The font's versatility allows it to be used for various purposes, from headlines to body text, while maintaining a cohesive and consistent brand identity.
